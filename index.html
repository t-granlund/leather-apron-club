The Credo of the Club

as it is engraved in the grove, and spoken at every sitting

  1. Ask better questions than the ones you are given.
  2. Improve each other honestly; accountability, never blame.
  3. Build for the town first. The barbershop, the artisan, the drone pilot.
  4. Leave it better than we found it.

The Standing Questions

after Dr. Franklin’s own, put to every member at every meeting

  • I. What new thing have you made, mended, or learned in your trade since we last sat — and what did it teach?
  • II. Hath any citizen, or any shop among us, lately met with difficulty in their undertakings — and how might the council assist?
  • III. What defect have you observed in the tools of our town — and who among us is best placed to mend it?
  • IV. Where did you last leave something better than you found it?
